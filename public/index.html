Good to know

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Sri Lanka?

Sri Lanka is a year-round destination because it has two monsoons. From December to April the west and south coasts and the hill country are best. From May to September the east coast, the north and the Cultural Triangle are best. Holiday in Lanka plans your route around the season you travel in.

Can I travel Sri Lanka independently without a travel agent?

Yes. Holiday in Lanka gives independent travellers a realistic day-by-day itinerary, verified stays and transport at fixed prices, and 24/7 support, so you get the safety of an agent with the freedom of planning yourself.

How long does it take to travel between places in Sri Lanka?

Distances look short on a map, but roads are often slow and winding. For example, Kandy to Ella takes about 4 to 5 hours by road. Our itineraries include buffer time for traffic and rest stops.

How many days do I need in Sri Lanka?

10 to 14 days is ideal for a first visit, covering the Cultural Triangle, the hill country and one coast. A week is enough for one or two regions at a relaxed pace.
