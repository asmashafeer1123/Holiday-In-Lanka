FAQ

Quick answers

How much baggage can I bring?

Every fare includes 20kg of checked baggage and one 7kg cabin bag. Extra kilos can be added in Manage Booking at LKR 650 per kg.

When does check-in close?

Online check-in closes 60 minutes before departure. Airport counters close 45 minutes before departure for all domestic flights.

Can I change my travel date?

Classic and Flex fares can be changed up to 24 hours before departure. Saver fares can be changed for a fee of LKR 3,500 plus any fare difference.

Do I need my passport for a domestic flight?

Sri Lankan citizens can travel with their NIC. Foreign nationals must present a valid passport at check-in.

Do you offer wheelchair assistance?

Yes — it's free. Request it while booking or contact us at least 48 hours before your flight so we can arrange it.
